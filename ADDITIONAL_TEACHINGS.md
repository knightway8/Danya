# Additional teachings and PDF sources

Checked September 4, 2026. Six additional publicly accessible PDFs contain Daniel Naroditsky's own writing. They are listed as **official-hosted sources, not verified open-license publications**. The new additions are links and editorial notes; the PDFs themselves have not been added to this repository.

## Three forewords in his own voice

| Publication | Where to find Danya's contribution | Why it belongs here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Chess Training for Candidate Masters - 17-page publisher sample](./Links/README.md#l154) | Foreword, printed pp. 7-9; PDF pp. 1-3. Signed November 22, 2016. The book is by Alexander Kalinin. | Danya describes reviewing every tournament game with his coach, including his title-clinching win, and continuing to consult their collection of instructive games. He explicitly praises the book's training material. |
| [Everyone's First Chess Workbook - 25-page publisher sample](./Links/README.md#l156) | Foreword, printed pp. 7-9; PDF pp. 3-5. Signed May 2021. The workbook is by Peter Giannatos. | He explains the gap between solving a prompted puzzle and recognizing the opportunity during a game. He endorses structured practice, written solutions, and revisiting exercises. |
| [Become a Chess Champion, Volume 2, Definitive Edition - Palm Beach Chess PDF](./Links/README.md#l157) | Foreword, printed/PDF pp. 6-8. Signed October 28, 2016. The file's cover credits Bryan Tillis. | He discusses the difficulty of teaching beginners and praises instruction that explains a broader set of ideas than isolated puzzles. The 192-page document is a book by Tillis, with a foreword by Danya. |

The two New In Chess samples are linked from their respective [Kalinin](./Links/README.md#l153) and [Giannatos](./Links/README.md#l155) product pages. The workbook sample's title page says **2021 and 738 exercises**, whereas the current product page advertises **759 exercises**. This guide identifies the sample actually inspected, without merging the editions. The Palm Beach PDF's contents lists Danya's foreword at page 5, but the actual foreword starts on page 6; use the page references above.

## Two further reading sources

[Jennifer Shahade's US Chess interview, March 22, 2010](./Links/README.md#l161) records Danya discussing handwritten analysis, forming his own opinion before using an engine, learning from losses, and retaining enjoyment of chess. His answers are a useful first-person teaching source. The article's superlative headline is its original title, not a separately verified historical record claim. The web reader retrieved the interview, while the direct automated request returned HTTP 403; access may vary.

[Inside Daniel Naroditsky's Childhood Journal](./Links/README.md#l162) is a later Chess.com article, displayed as updated June 29, 2026, under the byline `stingo1964`. It includes selected notebook images and reporting about their history. The article says the family requested that the complete journal not be publicly released. This repository links the published article only and does not reproduce the notebook images or seek the unpublished journal. Credit the article's commentary to its author, not to Danya.

## Availability and reuse

No explicit open license or general redistribution permission was established for these six PDFs. A free download and an open license are different things. For example, [New In Chess's copyright notice](./Links/README.md#l163) explicitly includes digital downloads among its protected content.

The same limitation applies to the earlier verification of the PDFs already in the folder: matching an official download proves file identity, not permission to re-upload it to GitHub. Existing files have been left unchanged. This follow-up adds official source links rather than additional mirrored publications.

All six new PDF downloads completed successfully and were parsed locally. Their relevant pages were visually inspected. The [supplementary verification record](./Links/additional_source_checks_2026-09-04.json) records page counts, hashes, and the scope of review. See the [link report](./Links/LINK_CHECK_REPORT.md) for the combined check results. This search is not an exhaustive bibliography.
