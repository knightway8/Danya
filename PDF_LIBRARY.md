## Additional PDFs available from their original hosts

The [additional teaching guide](./ADDITIONAL_TEACHINGS.md) identifies six more official-hosted PDFs containing Danya's own contributions: three forewords and three magazine issues with his game annotations. It gives exact page references and distinguishes his contribution from each publication's other authors. These six documents are linked, not stored here, and no open license or GitHub redistribution permission was verified.

## Preservation status

All five local PDFs are recorded in the manifest. The original four matched publisher-hosted files in the September 4, 2026 verification; the fifth is the unchanged publisher download saved on that date. A file hash helps detect later changes or corruption; it is not a statement about republication rights or the correctness of every chess variation.

The Chess.com ZIP still contains links rather than offline article copies. No new full-text Chess.com article PDFs have been created. There was no verified official PDF edition of those articles identified in this review. The [online resources area](./Links/README.md) records the source addresses and current check results.

Keep a second copy of this folder in a separate backup location to protect the locally preserved files as well as the catalog.
