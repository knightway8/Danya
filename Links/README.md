The 2010 US Chess interview was readable through the web reader despite the direct-request block; 29 YouTube destinations still have unverified playback.

“Reachable” means the page responded at check time. “Playback unverified” means a YouTube page responded; this does not prove the video can be watched. “Access varies” identifies the interview whose direct request was blocked despite content being available through the web reader. No status guarantees future access. The checks do not validate every account restriction or page fragment.
